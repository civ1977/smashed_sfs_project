2. What SMASHED – SFs is

SMASHED – SFs is an independently developed system that helps teachers, registrars, and school heads record grades and attendance and generate school forms (SF9, SF10, SF2, and related reports) in the format DepEd already requires. It has been introduced to and used by schools within the DepEd Division of Quezon, but it is not an official DepEd information system, and DepEd does not operate, warrant, or take responsibility for it. It is a tool that assists school staff in preparing these documents — it does not replace your school's own record-keeping obligations or DepEd's own official systems.

3. Account types and eligibility

The System has four kinds of accounts:

  • Class Adviser — full access to their own section's roster, grades, and reports.
  • Subject Teacher — grade entry and gradesheet access for the subjects they teach.
  • Non-Teaching / Registrar / Principal — school-wide record, account, and backup management.
  • Student (Learner Portal) — read-only access to their own ratings, free of charge.

Teacher and staff accounts are self-registered but should only be created by someone actually employed in that role at a real DepEd school. Student accounts require approval from their adviser or registrar before they become active. You may not create an account impersonating another person, or maintain more than one account for yourself.

4. Your account and password

You're responsible for the accuracy of the information you register with, and for keeping your password confidential. Don't share your login with anyone else, including other staff at your school — each person who needs access should have their own account, so actions can be tracked back to the person who took them. Tell us right away if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

5. Fees and payment

Student/Learner Portal accounts are free. Staff accounts (Class Adviser, Subject Teacher, Non-Teaching) carry a per-term fee at the rate shown on the Pricing page at the time you register, which may change from one term to the next with notice posted on that page. Payment is currently arranged directly with us through the channel we provide at registration, not automatically processed within the System — keep your proof of payment. An account with fees unpaid for the current term may be limited or suspended until payment is received; we'll make reasonable efforts to notify you first. Fees already paid for a term are non-refundable except where we've made an error or the System was unavailable to you for a significant part of that term through our fault.

8. Accuracy of generated forms

SF9, SF10, SF2, and every other report the System generates are built directly from the grades, attendance, and student information that you and your colleagues enter. We don't independently verify that data. Before printing, certifying, or officially submitting any document generated by the System, it's your responsibility — and your school's — to check it for accuracy. The System is an aid to preparing these documents, not a substitute for the judgment and certification responsibilities of the school official signing them.
