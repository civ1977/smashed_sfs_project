Tutorials

Get started in a few simple steps.

Step-by-step video walkthroughs are on the way. In the meantime, here's how to get going depending on your role.

For Advisers & Subject Teachers

  1. Register and complete your profile

    Create your teacher account, then select your school and set up your section under Complete Profile.

  2. Upload your students

    From your dashboard, upload your class list so every learner is on file with their LRN.

  3. Enter grades and mark attendance

    Use the Ratings and Attendance pages to record grades per term and daily attendance per section.

  4. Arrange your subjects (optional)

    Group subjects into Core and Elective, and set the order they appear in on the gradesheet, SF9, and SF10.

  5. Print your reports

    Generate SF9, SF10, rankings, and report cards from the Reports tab whenever you need them.

For Students

  1. Register with your LRN

    Sign up using your Learner Reference Number — it has to match a record your adviser has already uploaded.

  2. Wait for approval

    Your school registrar or adviser reviews and approves new student accounts before you can log in.

  3. View your ratings

    Once approved, log in anytime to see your Learner's Academic Ratings, updated as your teachers enter grades.

Common questions

I forgot my password, or my account was deactivated

Reach out to your school's registrar or the adviser who manages your account — they can reset access from their side. Registrars and principals can also reach us directly through Contact Us.

A page isn't loading or a form looks wrong

Try refreshing first — if it still doesn't look right, let us know through Contact Us with a screenshot if you can.
