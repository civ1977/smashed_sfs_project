Why a fee now?

The original SMASHED – SFs was a free Excel file that ran on your own computer. The Online Edition runs on a live cloud server, with the hosting and maintenance costs that come with keeping it fast and available to every school using it. The per-term fee covers that, at rates kept as low as possible: a Subject Teacher pays half of what a Class Adviser pays, reflecting the smaller scope of access.
