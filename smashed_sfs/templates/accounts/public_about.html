About

Built by a teacher, for teachers.

SMASHED – SFs (Spreadsheets of Microsoft Acting as Systems for Homeroom and Educational Digitalization of School Forms) started in 2021 as a personal tool built by a Senior High School adviser to survive quarterly grading season — and grew into a system now used by advisers, registrars, and school heads across the DepEd Division of Quezon.

Recognized DepEd School Innovation

Where it started

SMASHED – SFs was created by Victorino P. Pinawin, a teacher at Gumaca National High School, to automate the generation of SF9 and SF10 report cards for his own advisory class. As word spread, other teachers at GNHS asked for a copy, and the tool was publicly introduced to the Senior High School department in 2021.

It was later presented to San Francisco B NHS, Talipan NHS, and Villa Perez NHS — with Villa Perez National High School becoming the first school outside GNHS to adopt it. Since then, it has continued to grow one recommendation at a time, including an in-service training (INSET) session for teachers in Tayabas City in January 2024 and a division-wide introduction across DepEd Quezon's Senior High Schools in February 2024.

From spreadsheet to cloud system

The original SMASHED – SFs was a macro-enabled Microsoft Excel workbook — powerful, but tied to a single Windows computer and prone to file-sharing headaches between an adviser and their subject teachers. This site is the Online Edition: the same automated report generation, rebuilt as a cloud-based system so teachers and advisers can collaborate on the same records from any device, and students can view their own term ratings in real time.

It's no longer limited to Senior High School either — the current system supports Grade 4 through Grade 12.

Our mission

Give teachers back the hours they lose to manual encoding, so that time goes to actual teaching and supporting learners instead of paperwork.
