Per DepEd Order No. 015, s. 2026: the Academic Award column is the Academic Excellence Award (Annex H para 4) — GA of at least 90, with no Final Grade below 80 in any subject. Eligible learners must also have no derogatory records or disciplinary cases within the school year, which this app has no data for, so confirm that against actual records before finalizing. Rank is shown for the class's own records, but per the Order, awardees themselves should be announced/listed alphabetically rather than by rank. Excellence in a Specific Learning Area (Annex H item b, shown below the last learner) goes to whoever has the highest Final Grade in that subject, provided it's at least 90 — ties are all recognized. Both awards are computed only across your own advisory section, so if this grade level has other sections at your school, cross-check schoolwide before finalizing. Remarks is system-generated and only fires for the one non-obvious case: a GA of 90+ that still misses the Academic Excellence Award because of a subject below 80.
