Academic Excellence Award (Annex H para 4): GA of at least 90, with no Final Grade below 80 in any subject. Eligible learners must also have no derogatory records or disciplinary cases within the school year, which this app has no data for, so confirm that against actual records before finalizing. Per the Order, awardees should be announced/listed alphabetically rather than by rank.

Excellence in a Specific Learning Area (Annex H item b): whoever has the highest Final Grade in a subject, provided it's at least 90 - ties are all recognized. Unlike the per-section view on Summary of Ratings, this page pools every section in the grade level together, which is what actually makes it the DepEd "batch."
